Government agencies handle extraordinarily sensitive information that demands rigorous protection standards. From intelligence reports and diplomatic communications to citizen data and operational plans, these documents require classification systems that balance security requirements with operational accessibility. Poor document management creates vulnerabilities that adversaries actively exploit to compromise national security interests.
