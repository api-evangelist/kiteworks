Manufacturing supply chains face unprecedented data security challenges as digital transformation accelerates and cyber threats evolve. Modern production networks span multiple tiers of suppliers, contractors, and partners, creating complex data flows that traditional security perimeters cannot adequately protect. The stakes couldn’t be higher.
