Manufacturing firms across Oman manage extensive engineering documentation containing sensitive intellectual property, proprietary designs, and critical operational data. These organisations face mounting pressure to protect confidential information whilst maintaining collaboration with global partners, suppliers, and regulatory bodies. Security breaches targeting engineering documentation can expose trade secrets, compromise competitive advantage, and trigger significant regulatory compliance penalties.
