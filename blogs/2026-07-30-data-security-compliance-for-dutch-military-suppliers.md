Dutch military suppliers face increasingly complex data security requirements as defence contracts demand stricter protection of sensitive operational data, technical specifications, and classified information. These organisations must navigate multi-layered compliance frameworks while maintaining operational efficiency and competitive advantage in a highly regulated sector. The challenge extends beyond basic cybersecurity measures.
