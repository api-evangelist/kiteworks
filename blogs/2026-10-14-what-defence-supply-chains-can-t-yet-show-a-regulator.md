Defence supply chains supporting critical national infrastructure increasingly face both physical resilience obligations and cybersecurity requirements at once. An operator significant enough to be designated critical infrastructure is, in many jurisdictions, also significant enough to fall under essential-entity cybersecurity requirements simultaneously. The physical side gets proportionate attention because it produces visible, inspectable evidence.
