European banks face unprecedented pressure to maintain control over their data while leveraging cloud infrastructure for competitive advantage. Traditional approaches to cloud adoption often conflict with evolving regulatory expectations around data residency, operational resilience, and TPRM. Cloud sovereignty requirements now extend beyond simple geographic boundaries to encompass operational control, data lineage, and technological independence.
